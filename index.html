Goat Trail

Walk your twelve goats down the mountain to the pen before sundown, and fatten them up on the way.

On a phone, touch anywhere and slide your thumb like a joystick. With a mouse, hold to walk toward the cursor. Arrow keys or WASD work too. Goats step away from you and stick to the trail. Stride toward them to drive them; move slowly to slip past and get behind them.

Meadow is good. Clover is best. Flowers are tasty. Bitterweed makes goats sick, so herd them past it.
