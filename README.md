# Goat Trail

A small browser game about herding goats. Walk your twelve goats down the mountain to the pen before sundown, and fatten them up on the way.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step and nothing to install. The whole game is one HTML file using a canvas.

## Controls

- **Phone:** touch anywhere and slide your thumb like a joystick.
- **Mouse:** hold the button to walk toward the cursor.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD.

Goats step away from you and stick to the trail. Stride toward them to drive them, or move slowly to slip past and get behind them.

## Grazing

| Patch | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Meadow | Good |
| Clover | Best |
| Flowers | Tasty |
| Bitterweed | Makes goats sick and slow, so herd them past it |

## Scoring

You have 4½ minutes before sundown. Your score is out of 100. Half comes from how many goats reach the pen. The other half comes from how well fed those goats are. You earn up to three stars.
